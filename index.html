VERIFIED FEATURES

What Nucleora actually does

Every feature listed here exists in the codebase and runs on real engines. No vaporware.

🧬

Codon optimization

Re-code a protein for any of 30+ target species — including conservation species with real or clade-proxy codon tables from NCBI RefSeq coding sequences.

codon.py · python_codon_tables 0.1.18
⚛️

mRNA construct design

Assemble T7 promoter, 5′UTR, Kozak, signal peptide, antigen ORF, 3′UTR and poly(A) into a complete mRNA construct with part-level provenance.

mrna.py · design_misc2_mrna.py
🌊

RNA folding (ViennaRNA)

Compute secondary structure, minimum free energy, and dsRNA/hairpin immunogenicity risk with the ViennaRNA 2.7.2 engine. All folding runs locally.

design_misc2_rnafold.py · ViennaRNA 2.7.2
🏭

IVT simulation

Walk the construct through an in-vitro transcription run. Get a manufacturability report, predicted yield and kinetics, and an itemized order form.

ivt.py · vlab_ivt_simulator_core.py
✂️

Cloning & restriction

Restriction mapping, Gibson assembly, Golden Gate with overhang fidelity scoring (Potapov 2018 ligation data), and primer design with built-in Tm/GC fallback.

cloning.py · golden_gate_fidelity.py · primers.py
🔬

Virtual lab bench

Agarose gel simulation, in-silico PCR, peptide properties, pairwise/multiple alignment, Sanger/NGS sequencing QC, and 44 interactive lab experiments.

analysis_gel_*.py · labbench.py · analysis_align_*.py
🔌

CRISPR guide design

Guide RNA design with off-target scoring, HDR template generation, prime editing pegRNA design, library screens, and delivery vehicle selection.

crispr_guide_design_core.py · crispr_misc_hdr.py
💉

Vaccination studio

Guided one-click workflow for animal vaccine design — antigen selection through codon optimization, mRNA assembly, manufacturing simulation, and allometric dose scaling.

vaccination_studio.py · allometry.py
📁

Import & export

FASTA, GenBank, GFF3 export. SBOL support (best-effort). Project management with construct history, versioning, and provenance tracking across sessions.

platform_import_export_core.py · formats.py
CONSERVATION FOCUS

30+ species, from real RefSeq data

Codon choice has to match the animal being dosed. Nucleora ships real codon tables built from NCBI RefSeq coding sequences. Where a species has insufficient data, it uses a documented clade-anchored proxy — and names it.

HOW IT WORKS

Three steps, one window

The guided workflow takes you from antigen to exportable mRNA construct. Every step runs locally.

1

Find your antigen

Search UniProt and NCBI Protein for your target sequence. Filter by species, strain, or isolate. The accession and organism metadata travel with it into the final export.

2

Design the mRNA

Codon-optimize for your target species, then assemble the full mRNA construct: cap, UTRs, Kozak, signal peptide, CDS, poly(A). Fold with ViennaRNA. Simulate the IVT run.

3

Review & export

Review quality metrics (CAI, GC%, CpG). Check the manufacturability report. Export as FASTA, GenBank, or GFF3. Generate a synthesis order form.

HONEST ABOUT LIMITS

What Nucleora is, and what it isn't

What Nucleora is

  • A design workbench for DNA and mRNA built on public reference genomes (NCBI, UniProt).
  • Local-first — Flask app on localhost:8770. Your sequences never leave your machine.
  • Method-transparent — every number traces to a named, published algorithm or data source.
  • Cross-platform — macOS and Windows, with native launcher scripts for both.
  • Built on real engines — ViennaRNA 2.7.2, BioPython 1.87, scipy, numpy, pandas.

What it isn't

  • Not a medical device. Not a source of clinical or diagnostic advice.
  • Not a wet-lab substitute — designs need real validation at the bench.
  • Not a data-harvesting cloud — nothing leaves your computer unless you fetch a public record.
  • Not publicly released yet — available by early-access request only.
  • Not a substitute for IBC review — constructs for cells/animals/humans require proper approval.
